      Shipping FAQs

      How long does a national shipment take?

      24 to 72 hours depending on destination. Shipments within the same city are usually delivered the same day.

      What do I need to send money?

      Your ID (DIP or passport) and the recipient's name and phone number.

      How do I pay?

      In cash at any of our offices. Ask us about other options in your city.

      I have lost my tracking number

      Message us on WhatsApp with your name and the shipping date and we will find it.

      Is there a weight limit?

      Up to 30 kg per parcel for standard shipping. For heavier items, ask for a quote.
